Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

EchoFlow is a desktop voice assistant for Windows. This policy explains what data the app processes and what it does not.

Data we process on your device

  • Microphone audio — captured only while you actively hold the push-to-talk shortcut or use tap-to-speak. Audio from that session is converted to text and is not kept.
  • Transcripts, aliases, macros, and command history — stored locally on your computer. Command history is never uploaded to an EchoFlow-operated server.
  • Connected apps list — names of apps you connect in the Hub, stored locally.

Language service

When a command cannot be handled on your PC, EchoFlow may send the transcript (not the audio) to its language service to understand what you asked.

What we do not do

  • No always-on microphone listening
  • No screen reading or keystroke logging outside voice sessions
  • No EchoFlow account or cloud sync required
