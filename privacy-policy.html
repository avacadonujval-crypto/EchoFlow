EchoFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

EchoFlow is a desktop voice assistant for Windows. This policy explains what data the app processes and what it does not.

Data we process on your device

  • Microphone audio — captured only while you actively hold the push-to-talk shortcut or use tap-to-speak. Audio from that session is converted to text and is not kept.
  • Transcripts, aliases, macros, and command history — stored locally on your computer. Command history is never uploaded to an EchoFlow-operated server.
  • Service credentials — stored only on this PC, and never shown in the app.

Data sent over the network

When a spoken command cannot be handled on this PC, EchoFlow may send the transcript text only to its language service. Audio is not uploaded.

EchoFlow does not operate a backend that receives your audio or transcripts.

Permissions

  • Microphone — for push-to-talk dictation and commands
  • Accessibility-equivalent input simulation — to paste text and trigger system shortcuts

EchoFlow does not read or log screen content.

Always-on listening

EchoFlow does not offer always-on background listening in the current version. Listening starts only when you hold the configured shortcut or press tap-to-speak.

Contact

For privacy questions about your installation, review Settings → Privacy inside the app, or contact the distributor who provided this build.
